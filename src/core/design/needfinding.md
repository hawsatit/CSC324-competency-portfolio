# 3.1 Needfinding

**Artifact:** <https://github.com/grinnell-csc324-fall-2026/recycling/tree/main/docs>

## Context and purpose

- The intended users were Grinnell College students.
- I interviewed my roommate during the needfinding.
- The biggest problem identified was the lack of knowledge of recycling locations and how to recycle.

## Evidence of mastery

- I created interview questions that would help answer questions about the needs of our program.
- Direct quotes from the interview support the identified needs.
- The interviews shifted the project goals toward displaying ways to recycle, things to recycle, and instructions/directions.

## My contribution

Did an interview with my roommate and discussed the results with my group.

## Reflection

I thought this was a pretty fun experience, as initially I didn't really care about the goal of this project, but interviewing my friends showed that recycling was actually a very hard thing to do in Grinnell. It was also cool to see how this process, which seems so informal, can give very valuable information, and I am curious to see how large companies implement needfinding.
