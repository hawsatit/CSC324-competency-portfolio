# 3.4 Evaluation

**Artifact:** We used our wireframe and Wizard of Oz prototypes to interview potential users.

## Context and purpose

- We wanted to see how people might interact with our initial interface design.

## Evidence of mastery

- We used our prototype and guided people through it.
- I interviewed and took notes.
- This allowed me to identify what features users think are important, and what buttons they would click.

## My contribution

I used our wireframe prototype and conducted an interview.

## Reflection

I think this exercise was especially useful to see what features the users would actually use. In my interview, the user mentioned that if they were already on our website, then they already have the motivation to recycle, so having information on why to recycle might not be as helpful. They also mentioned it would be more useful to quickly go through choosing the item they want to recycle and directly see the instructions, and after that, their usage of the website is over. This emphasizes the importance of ease of use, and a design that doesn't confuse the user (e.g. making certain buttons/info larger).
