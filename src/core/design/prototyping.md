# 3.3 Prototyping

**Artifact:** Wizard of Oz and wireframe prototypes — <https://github.com/grinnell-csc324-fall-2026/recycling/tree/main/docs>

## Context and purpose

- The purpose of this prototype was to assess user needs and how a user might interact with our interface.

## Evidence of mastery

- I designed the prototype of our main and results pages.
- I took our prototype to interviews to see what I can change to make it easier to use.
- The prototype allowed us to see the importance of the sizing of different elements so users can know what to click and do.

## My contribution

I helped design the prototype and do the interviews with it.

## Reflection

At first, I thought a paper prototype is kind of silly because it isn't really realistic of what our website is intended to be. After doing it, something I realized was very helpful was the freedom we have to just draw out what we think would be a good design for our website. By doing it by hand, we have the freedom to draw out anything without the restrictions of using a tool like Canva. It also allowed us to get our ideas out into something physical so everyone in our group could see our vision.
