# 3.2 Ideation

**Artifact:** Mind Map, Sketch Storm, Worst Possible Idea — <https://github.com/grinnell-csc324-fall-2026/recycling/tree/main/docs>

## Context and purpose

- The purpose was to combine our needfinding results to identify key themes and develop the main goals for our program.

## Evidence of mastery

- The mind map helped organize our thoughts to identify themes, worst possible idea helped us brainstorm a bunch of things not to do, and sketch storm helped us come up with design possibilities for the UI.
- For UI design, we took our main goals from the brainstorm and came up with sketches that highlight the main features we think are the most important.
- We ended up with the final UI design that we think will be most effective.

## My contribution

Helped brainstorm and draw on the whiteboard.

## Reflection

I thought this process was very useful to put all our thoughts together to identify key themes, which allowed us to narrow down what our goal was. When we were doing the mind map, the lines we drew got us thinking about how different goals connected, and what we need to implement in order to achieve all the goals. I thought the worst possible idea activity was interesting because I've never done that before. It allowed me to think through things that we shouldn't do and problems that might come up with our design.
